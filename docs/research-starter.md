# Research reading queue

These are starting references, not a completed related-work review. Read each primary
source and record its contribution, limitations, implementation implications and decisions.
Model cards and dataset schema were checked during initial setup; the papers below are
queued for full study and bibliographic verification before the report is submitted.

1. TrialGPT original implementation/paper entry point: https://github.com/ncbi-nlp/TrialGPT
2. Mandatory data: https://huggingface.co/datasets/ncbi/TrialGPT-Criterion-Annotations
3. Attention Is All You Need: https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762
4. LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models: https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685
5. FlashAttention: Fast and Memory-Efficient Exact Attention with IO-Awareness: https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.14135
6. Fast Inference from Transformers via Speculative Decoding: https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.17192
7. Hugging Face cache documentation: https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers/en/cache_explanation
8. Qwen target model card: https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct
9. Qwen draft/alternative model card: https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct

For each major decision record: alternatives, reason selected, measured evidence,
observed failure, and what changed. Do not claim a speedup or a clinical quality level
without a reproducible experiment.
